
## Advent of Code 

[Advent of Code](https://adventofcode.com) has become some sort of a winter tradition for me. I was first introduced to it by some guy on Discord in November of 2021, and on the subsequent month, I started my first AoC season, the 2021 season- and immediately was hooked into the culture. I ended up participating in every season since, never managing to complete it (yet), but trying until it gets too hard for me, at which point I stop.

I love the community aspect, all of us coming together at midnight EST, anxiously waiting for the clock to reach 00:00 so we could refresh the page and get started on the new puzzle. The feverish feeling knowing that someone else could be typing the exact some keys as you at the exact same time somewhere else in the world brings some kind of joy to me. And after the puzzle- going on Reddit and seeing some of the crazy solutions, and the people who solved it in some obscure ways, or set some crazy challenges for themselves, has always been a source of inspiration for me.

This repository serves as an archive containing all attempts to solve the puzzles. Most of them were solved on the day of, under 24 hours. Huge thanks to [Eric Wastl](https://was.tl/), without whom Advent of Code would not exist (quite literally)
